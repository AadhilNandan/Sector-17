### 2. Scientific Instrument Package References

The engineering specifications and telemetry models in the Spacecraft Workshop are grounded in real instrument packages developed for deep space Jovian exploration:

1. **REASON (Radar for Europa Assessment and Sounding: Ocean to Near-surface):**
   - In-game designation: `ICE-PENETRATING RADAR (IPR-3)`
   - Science context: Dual-frequency ice-penetrating radar probing through Europa's outer icy shell to detect subsurface water interfaces.
2. **MASPEX (Mass Spectrometer for Planetary Exploration):**
   - In-game designation: `HIGH-RESOLUTION MASS SPECTROMETER (MASPEX)`
   - Science context: High-mass-resolution gas chromatography and time-of-flight spectrometry analyzing volatiles and organics.
3. **Magnetometer (Interior Characterization):**
   - In-game designation: `MAGNETIC INDUCTION BOOM (MAG-BOOM)`
   - Science context: Measuring induced magnetic dipole fields in Europa's subsurface ocean interacting with Jupiter's magnetosphere.
4. **PIMS (Plasma Instrument for Magnetic Sounding):**
   - In-game designation: `PLASMA FLUX SPECTROMETER (PIMS)`
   - Science context: Correcting plasma currents to determine ocean conductivity and thickness.

### 3. Planetary Textures & Astrodynamic Data

- **Jupiter Planetary Map:** NASA/JPL-Caltech Solar System Simulator (`maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/`).
- **Europa Surface Map:** NASA/JPL/USGS Voyager and Galileo global mosaics.
- **Astrodynamics Constants:** NASA JPL Horizons system planetary ephemerides and gravitational parameters ($\mu_{\text{Jupiter}} = 1.266865 \times 10^{17}\,\text{m}^3/\text{s}^2$, $\mu_{\text{Europa}} = 3.20273 \times 10^{12}\,\text{m}^3/\text{s}^2$).
